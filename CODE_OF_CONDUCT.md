# Code of Conduct

Mypack follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good intent, keep criticism about the work and not
the person, and make it easy for newcomers to take part. Harassment, insults
and personal attacks are not accepted in issues, pull requests, discussions or
any other project space.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour is a problem, contact the maintainers privately through
the repository's private vulnerability reporting form (mark it "Conduct") or by
messaging a maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, and temporarily or
permanently block people who don't follow this code.
